MARINATED GRILLED ARTICHOKES

BUILDING ON THE VAST EXPERIENCE OF GIVREX IN SELECTING AND PROCESSING THE DELICATE MOST PREMIUM ARTICHOKES WITH STEM, THE MOST TENDER AND TASTY ARTICHOKES ARE SELECTED FOR THIS DELICIOUS READY TO HEAT AND EAT PRODUCT.

After careful selection of the artichokes with stem, they are cut into halves and marinated in a special recipe of the finest ingredients developed exclusively for GIVREX by its own internationally experienced Chef.

The marinated artichokes are then skillfully grilled and frozen. The GRILLED MARINATED HALVES OF ARTICHOKE WITH STEM are tender at heart, quite delicious and suitable for vegan.
