IQF FROZEN GREEN PEAS AND CARROTS
INGREDIENTS: SMALL GREEN PEAS AND YOUNG CARROTS. ONE OF THE MOST CONSUMED VEGETABLES MIXTURE.

Green peas are of first choice. They are carefully sorted and selected. They are immediately processed and frozen in order to prevent forming starch and to keep their natural characteristics as well.

Carrots are derived from young carrots chosen among the sweetest varieties. After strictly selecting the most tender ones, the carrots are washed, peeled, and water is removed before this product is immediately frozen. By this, they keep all their nutritive and taste characteristics.
