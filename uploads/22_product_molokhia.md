FROZEN MOLOKHIA
MOLOKHIA IS AN EGYPTIAN VEGETABLE NATURALLY RICH IN IRON. IT HAS DARK GREEN LEAVES.

Our product is obtained from the best varieties. Through its qualified experts, GIVREX looks for the best plantations specialized in producing the highest quality of this raw vegetable. This product is always appreciated and requested by the Egyptian consumers as well as by other consumers all over the world.

After we receive them, the molokhia leaves are treated in a particular way to preserve their high quality and the high level of sweetness that characterizes this product, which has a succulent taste after its production in our factory. This allows the preparation of a rich green soup that will satisfy the most discerning gourmets.
