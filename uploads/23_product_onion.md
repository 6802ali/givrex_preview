ONION
AT GIVREX WE RECEIVE THE STRONG SCENTED WHITE OR RED ONIONS HARVESTED FROM THE RICH SOIL OF THE NILE VALLEY.
Onions exude an overwhelmingly piercing odor and remarkably enduring taste because of its exceptional dry matter.
A favorite of Mediterranean and Oriental cuisine we offer the onions cut into dices or slices ready for application in cooking.

- Onion Dices: Selected for a strong odor and remarkable taste, diced onion is ready to use in cooking same as fresh onion.
- Onion Slices: After removing of the excess roots, onions are sliced to 2-6 cm thickness.
