FROZEN SEMI FRIED FALAFEL
OUR CHEF'S OWN TOUCH TO THE TRADITIONAL (ETHNIC) RECIPE OF THE POPULAR EGYPTIAN FALAFEL GIVES THE GIVREX FALAFEL A DELICIOUS TASTE.

Carefully selected ingredients, garlic, fresh herbs and condiments are skillfully minced into a homogenous paste, formed into uniform patties and semi-fried until golden brown. The frying process is closely controlled by our experienced staff. This product is ready to heat and eat. Fried falafel are commonly served in sandwiches with tahini sauce and salad. GIVREX falafel is available in three variants each offering a distinct mouth-watering taste. All of them suitable for a vegan diet.

- Falafel (Fava Beans & Chickpeas): made mainly of dried fava beans and chickpeas, minced with vegetables, freshly harvested herbs and condiments.
- Falafel (Chickpeas): made mainly of chickpeas, minced with vegetables, freshly harvested herbs and condiments.
- Falafel (Fava Beans): made mainly of dried fava beans, minced with vegetables, freshly harvested herbs and condiments.
