IQF FROZEN SWEET POTATOES
WE OFFER AT GIVREX PARTICULAR SWEET POTATOES because Givrex looks at how to harvest it in the fertile land of the Nile valley, which is very rich in silt and gravel. This allows Givrex to obtain uniform beautiful tubers untwisted.

- Sweet Potato Dices: The sweet potatoes are carefully peeled and uniformly cut, blanched and frozen. The final product will give a shiny color to your dishes.
- Sweet Potato Sliced: The uncoated fingers of sweet potatoes are semi-fried in controlled temperature and frozen. We recommend this product to be heated in an air-fryer or in a pan with oil and served hot. It may be presented as a colorful side to any dish or as a nutritious and tasty snack.
