GRILLED VEGETABLES

Grilled Eggplants / Aubergine
Fresh dark and shiny eggplant / aubergines are chosen for this product. The nicely curved eggplant / aubergines are washed, sliced in a uniform thickness and grilled to obtain visible grill marks on each slice then frozen.

Grilled Zucchini
Fresh bright green zucchini are washed, carefully sliced and grilled on both sides to obtain visible grill marks on each slice then frozen. With the GIVREX expertise, the subtle taste of zucchini is well preserved.
