FROZEN VINE LEAVES
OUR VINE LEAVES ARE FROM VINES PRODUCING GREEN GRAPES WITHOUT SEEDS. THESE LEAVES HAVE A SMOOTH AND SILKY TOUCH AND A CLEAR GREEN COLOR.

Only the soft leaves are preserved. The rough ones are removed as they contain many filaments.
This product is processed with the know-how which protects the leaves against any freeze burn.
