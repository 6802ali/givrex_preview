IQF RED FIGS
OUR FIGS ARE PREPARED USING FRESH FIGS, WASHED AND CUT IN QUARTERS ALONG THE LENGTH OF THE FRUIT.
Grown under the Egyptian sun, they are harvested when ripe, then selected by hand. They can be used to prepare many types of desserts such as pies, compotes and jams. They can also be an interesting ingredient in a fruit salad.
