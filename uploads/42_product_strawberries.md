IQF STRAWBERRIES
FULLY RED STRAWBERRIES WITH HIGHER BRIX: WHOLE CALIBRATED, CUT AND IN HALVES TO SUIT YOUR NEEDS.

Strawberries are supplied to GIVREX by specialized plantations which apply the best techniques known in the agricultural field during and after planting and at harvest. In general, all plantations apply the required care after the harvest according to the instructions stipulated by the BRC, ISO, HACCP and GLOBALG.A.P.

The strawberries processed within our factory must be ripe and of a red color from inside and outside. These strawberries contain a high level of sweetness.

Our strawberries are free from pesticides.

Moreover, all analysis operations are carried out regularly over all phases of production.
We provide strawberries whole calibrated, cut and in halves.
